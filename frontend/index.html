    Log formats

    Cabrillo (.log) – the standard contest submission format. QSO lines look like QSO: 10G CW 2026-08-15 1005 K2UA EN91KT KB8VAO EN91IS.

    CSV (.csv), or a Google Sheet – one row per QSO with the columns date, band, sourcegrid, time, call, grid:

    date,band,sourcegrid,time,call,grid
2026-08-15,10 GHz,EN91KT,1005,KB8VAO,EN91IS
,,,1032,W3XYZ,EN90OV
,24 GHz,,1145,K1ROV,EN90UX

    Leave date, band, sourcegrid and time blank when they haven’t changed since the previous row – they’re filled down automatically. call and grid must be on every row. Dates may be YYYY-MM-DD or MM/DD/YYYY; times are UTC HHMM.

    Maps

    Maps are drawn by K2UA’s open-source grid-mapper. CSV and Google Sheets logs are converted to Cabrillo first, so every map uses the grid you operated from on each QSO. Coastline and border data: Natural Earth; street underlay: © OpenStreetMap contributors.

    Scoring

    Distance points (km, rounded up, minimum 1) × band multiplier (10 GHz ×1, 24 ×2, 47 ×3, 78 ×4, 122 ×5, 142 ×6, 241+ ×10), plus 100 points per unique call per band.
